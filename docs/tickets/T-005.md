# T-005: Record settlements

Status: done
Milestone: M2
Risk: high
Issue: #17

## Goal

A member can record that they paid another member back with `tally settle <payer> <receiver> <amount>`, and balances reflect it. Recording every payment `tally settle-up` lists brings every balance to 0.

## Done when

1. In the example group (Ana 17.00, Bo −7.00, Cy −10.00), `tally settle Bo Ana 7.00` then `tally balances` prints Ana 10.00, Bo 0.00, Cy −10.00. Checked by `tests/test_cli.py::test_cli_settle_updates_balances`. (S-1)
2. `Ledger.add_settlement(payer, receiver, amount)` moves `balances()` by exactly `+amount` for the payer and `−amount` for the receiver, matches names case-insensitively and trimmed, and keeps the balances summing to 0. Checked by `tests/test_ledger.py::test_settlement_moves_balances`. (S-2)
3. An unknown payer, an unknown receiver, payer equal to receiver (including `ana` vs `Ana`), and amounts `0`, `-1`, `1.234` each raise a `TallyError` saying what was wrong and record nothing; the CLI exits 1 and the file's bytes are unchanged. Checked by `tests/test_ledger.py::test_invalid_settlements_record_nothing` and `tests/test_cli.py::test_cli_settle_errors_leave_the_file_unchanged`. (S-3)
4. On at least 300 seeded random ledgers (D-5), some with 13 or more members with a non-zero balance, recording every payment from `settle_up(ledger.balances())` as a settlement makes every balance 0. Checked by `tests/test_settle.py::test_recording_settle_up_clears_every_balance`. (S-4)
5. In the example group, running `tally settle` with each line `tally settle-up` prints makes `tally balances` print 0.00 for everyone. Checked by `tests/test_cli.py::test_cli_settling_every_settle_up_payment_clears_balances`. (S-5)
6. A settlement larger than what the payer owes is recorded: from Ana 17.00, Bo −7.00, `add_settlement("Bo", "Ana", 1000)` gives Bo 3.00, Ana 7.00. Checked by `tests/test_ledger.py::test_overpaying_settlement_is_recorded`. (S-15, D-12)
7. The full test command passes: `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q`. (S-14)

## Assumptions

- Interfaces come from the design: `Settlement(payer, receiver, amount)` with the same field names as `settle.Payment`; settlements are `entries` items with `"kind": "settlement"` (ADR 0003). The file format stays version 2: T-003's v2 reader accepts the `settlement` kind, added here.
- The settle CLI's help reads "record that <payer> paid <receiver> back"; amounts are parsed with `parse_amount`.
- Settlements carry no note in M2.
- Sensitive area: money calculations (balances summing to 0). Conditions 2, 4 and 6 cover it.
